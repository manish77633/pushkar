Glimpses of India

Our Glimpses of India tour takes you on a colorful journey through old Rajasthan in style, with good food and luxury accommodation on an engaging journey which takes you far from the tourist trail.

Along the way there will be opportunities to meet local people, glimpse the traditions and rhythms of daily life and taste the food of the region as you discover local customs, legends and celebrations.

You will stay in a 18th century painted painted mansion in Shekhawati and a rural farm in the beautiful Aravalli hills, experience local fairs and festivals, and find some of the real highlights of Rajasthan. Our unique properties are very much part of their local communities and feature home cooking based on regional recipes and organic produce from our farm.

Tour Itinerary

Glimpses of India

1

DELHI: We meet your flight and transfer you to the luxury of Delhi’s Imperial hotel for an overnight stay.

2

SHEKHAWATI: After breakfast travel the old trading roads to Shekhawati, a region with an extraordinary heritage of fresco paintings, where our carefully restored 1860s merchant haveli will be your home for the next three days. The village is known for its traditional rangrez dyers community and local crafts. Peacocks roam the gardens and streets, goats graze in abandoned mansions; watch the life of the local bazaars, relax in the garden with a glass of wine and enjoy a candlelit haveli dinner.

3

SHEKHAWATI: On a guided visit to a nearby town you will see some of the best frescoed havelis in the region and discover the history of this unusual region. Later in the day you may like to take a walk in the town with your host, enjoy bird watching or try a cooking session at the haveli.

4

ARAVALLI FARM: A short drive takes you to a working farm to meet a local family. This traditional farming area is full of interest and offers you fascinating insights into the life and customs of Rajasthan and a relaxing stay.

5

ARAVALLI FARM: Enjoy an Indian-style family picnic in fields of organically-cultivated ground nuts, fenugreek, wheat and barley and hear about local life.

6

JODHPUR: Vibrant Jodhpur, the ‘Blue City’ is famed for its spectacular fort, palaces and monuments, old markets and traditional bazaars; the winding lanes of the old city offer photographic opportunities.

7

JODHPUR: From the blue city travel on to Jaisalmer, the golden city, once the hub of caravan trading routes from the east.

8

JAISALMER: This ancient desert citadel built of honey-coloured stone began as a trading post on the caravan routes and ruled over a vast area of the Thar desert; one of the most romantic and evocative destinations in Rajasthan, an architectural and cultural treasure.

9

JAISALMER: Take a sightseeing tour of the fort, then ride a camel to see something of the area of desert villages and farms around Jaisalmer and find out how people find a balance with nature in this arid landscape.

10

JAISALMER: Transfer to the airport for a flight to Delhi.
