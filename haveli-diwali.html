Haveli Diwali and Pushkar Fair

Diwali in a Painted Haveli in Shekhawati

Join us for a luxury small group journey on less travelled routes, visiting the Golden Temple of Amritsar, following the old trade routes of the camel caravans to Bikaner, the desert citadel of Jaisalmer and the majestic fort and walled city of Jodhpur. Discover the landscapes and culture of the Thar Desert in out-of-the-way destinations and join us for Diwali celebrations in a beautiful old painted haveli in Shekhawati as you journey through centuries of Rajasthani culture to the iconic Pushkar Fair.

Offering a warm welcome and stylish travel, our longer Haveli Diwali tour is an ideal introduction to India if you like to get away from the crowds.

On the Haveli Diwali tour you will travel as part of a small group. Prices are based on two people sharing; if you are travelling on your own and would like help finding someone to share with, please enquire.

This Is one of our most popular tours with a limited number of places, so early booking is recommended.

23 Oct

DELHI On arrival, transfer to New Delhi’s renowned Imperial hotel for a warm welcome and an opportunity to meet fellow travellers.

24 Oct

AMRITSAR (Golden Temple) Take the Express train to Amritsar. Afternoon at leisure before an evening visit to the Golden Temple. All faiths are welcomed. Visit community kitchens which feed thousands daily and witness temple rituals as the Guru Granth Sahib, Holy Book of the Sikhs, is carried to the inner chamber for the night. Two night stay at the Taj hotel.

25 Oct

AMRITSAR Return to the Golden Temple as morning light plays on the waters. Join pilgrims in community services, serving tea, clearing utensils or preparing food, or explore lively bazaars nearby. After lunch, drive to Wagah for the evening ceremony at the border between India and Pakistan. Amritsar is renowned for its food, with speciality stalls and markets. Evening visit to one of the most famous old restaurants for a traditional Amritsari dinner.

26 Oct

PATIALA Drive to the former princely state of Patiala. See the Qila Mubarak, Darbar Hall, Sheesh Mahal (mirror palace) and Moti Bagh Palace, and the street tailors, who shelter from the sun and rain under umbrellas. Stay overnight at the Ran Baas The Palace hotel.

27 Oct

MUKUNDGARH Drive through rural villages to reach Mukundgarh, in Rajasthan’s fascinating Shekhawati region, for a three night stay in our beautifully restored, painted merchant havell.

28 Oct

MUKUNDGARH Take a walk in the village. Meet families engaged in bandhej tie-and-dye work, visit the library and Lakshmanirayan temple and ride in a tuk-tuk to see everyone preparing for the festival. Later, help us shop for Diwall and decorate the haveli with lights for the celebrations.

29 Oct

MUKUNDGARH Happy Diwalil Visit Lakshmangarh and Fatehpur or Nawalgarh to see beautiful frescoed havelis then drive back to Mukundgarh for the Diwali rituals and a traditional celebratory haveli dinner.

30 Oct

BIKANER Drive to Bikaner, one of the great trading posts of the old caravan routes. Stay at stylish Narendra Bhawan, a former palace.

31 Oct

BIKANER As well as one of the finest forts of Rajasthan there’s a fascinating bazaar, merchant havelis, temples and museums and the National Research Centre on Camel.

01 Nov

JAISALMER Follow the old trade route across the desert to Jaisalmer to stay for two nights at The Gulaab Hotel

02 Nov

JAISALMER

03 Nov

JODHPUR Drive to Jodhpur, visiting a anciant The Osiyan Jain Temple on the way. Your day is at leisure to explore the blue city and shop in the colorful bazaars around the clocktower. Two night stay at Pal Haveli Jodhpur.

04 Nov

JODHPUR A morning tour of the spectacular Mehrangarh Fort and the royal cenotaphs of Jaswant Thada.

05 Nov

PUSHKAR Drive to Pushkar for a three night stay in tented Resort. Rest of the day at leisure.

06 Nov

PUSHKAR Visit the fair to see camel herds, horses and traders arriving. The early days of the fair are less crowded but with plenty to see. Visit Pushkar Lake and the Brahma Temple and walk in the bazaars.

07 Nov

PUSHKAR You may hike up to Savitri Temple in the morning to take in spectacular views over the fair. Day in the fair. You may opt for a camel cart or hot air balloon ride in the morning or evening.

08 Nov

PUSHKAR Morning free to relax or make a last visit to the fair before checking out for departure for Delhi. or your next destination.

8 Nov

JAIPUR After breakfast drive to the Pink City, Jaipur, for a two night stay. Your day is free to visit the Pink City bazaars. In the evening drive up to Nahargarh Fort, high above the city with views over the Illuminations of Jaipur.

9 Nov

JAIPUR A day of guided sightseeing around the City Palace complex and Amber Fort. In the evening visit a farmhouse for a Rajasthani dinner.

10 Nov

AGRA Drive towards Agra visiting the 1000-year-old Abhaneri Stepwell on the way. Afternoon tour of Fatehpur Sikri, the abandoned citadel of the Mughal Emperor Akbar. Evening at leisure. Overnight stay in Agra.

11 Nov

AGRA-DELHI Sunrise guided tour of Taj Mahal. Later visit the historical Agra Fort before transferring to Delhi for a two night stay.

12 Nov

DELHI Daylong sightseeing of Old Delhi (Shahajahanabad) and Lutyen’s Delhi (New Delhi). Evening free to shop around the hub of New Delhi, Connaught Place.

13 Nov

DELHI Transfer to New Delhi international airport for departure to your onward destination.
