GLIMPSES OF INDIA

Journey through old Rajasthan in style: stay in a restored 1860s frescoed haveli in Shekhawati, share a family picnic on an Aravalli farm, then discover Jodhpur’s blue lanes and Jaisalmer’s golden fort.

HAVELI DIWALI TOUR

Celebrate Diwali the way Rajasthan has for generations at Mukunda Haveli, a restored 18th-century mansion in Shekhawati. Continue through Rajasthan’s historic desert cities and experience the spectacular Pushkar Camel Fair.
