Photography

Pushkar Camel Fair Photography

You are going to find many photographers around the dunes in the camels area at any point of the day. While this can be found annoying, the scene is attractive enough for any individual to capture in the he/she imagines. A lot happens in different directions all the time. Camels are large living things who tend to follow their own routine. They must be fed, taken around and cared. Plus, there are potential buyers examining them and sellers ready to explain their products. Its much similar or buying a car from the showroom. After a purchase, you would like to add good accessories and thus there are barbers who shave designs on the camels, tamers who would help in things like nose piercing and decorators who would sell the required ornamentation. In the camels area you are going to find some gypsy families living in temporarily built huts with flimsy enclosure to their forecourt. You will feel tempted to take pictures but they expect you take permission and pay some tips. Same is with beautifully dressed gypsy girls who roam around so that you click their pictures and make some payment. There are entertainers roaming around and sometimes they put up nice dance show over a dune. Feel free to appreciate, click pictures but remember to pay some amount as token of appreciation. You are hardly ever going to find any objection in taking endless pictures of camels and the men who are around them.

Marwari Horses

Pushkar is a great place for equine photography. Very beautiful and impressive Marwari horses are brought from faraway places especially Punjab. Even Kathiawadi horses are brought. You can spend a lot of time in various camps and everyone is warm and hospitable to those who take genuine interest. Many Hindu ashrams also bring their horses for display in the Fair.

Savitri Temple Viewpoint and Sadhus and Ghats

Climb upto Savitri Temple on one afternoon to catch the views of the entire sprawling scene glowing infront of you. The stadium too is a good place to linger on for some surprisingly good shots. Check the calender of events, some of them offer great photography material. Not to be missed are the scenes on the main bazaar around the holy lake and the little lanes close to them where there is a constant stream of pilgrims arriving and making their march towards their respective dharamshalas and the Ghats. These are the people belonging to different rural regions of Rajasthan and different castes and each such group has its own unique appearances. Pushkar is also a very good place to have the face photography of Sadhus of all kinds. Generally they are obliging on request. On the ghats, not to be missed are the deepdaan events. If there is free time after doing all of these, you may choose to visit the farmlands on Motisar Road to see the famous rose farming of Pushkar.
