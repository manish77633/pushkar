Pushkar Fair and Dev Diwali in Varanasi

Our Divine Diwali small group journey combines Pushkar Fair with one of India’s most moving and mystical experiences; watch from a riverboat as the ancient ghats of Varanasi are lit for the Dev Deepavali festival and countless earthen lamps are floated on the holy Ganges.

Along the way are opportunities to meet local people and pilgrims, discover India’s vibrant street culture as well as our more spiritual side, try regional food and participate In celebrations and worship. You will visit local temples, explore the temples and pilgrimage traditions linked to Pushkar Fair, join Kartik Purnima devotions, and witness an unforgettable expression of Diwali and Varanasi’s deep connection with the divine.

Our flexible small group journeys are designed to offer you an experience more like private touring. Whilst sharing transport and some experiences with other guests, you are still free to vary activities and follow your own interests. Travel is relaxed, in comfortable vehicles and our expert guides bring destinations alive. Prices are based on a group of eight people with two people sharing a room; If you are travelling on your own and would like help finding someone to share with, please enquire.

DATES: 5–15 November 2027

Tour Itinerary

Divine Diwali
Pushkar Fair and Varanasi’s Dev Diwali 2027

Tour Price from $4800 based on twin sharing. What it includes >

5 Nov

Delhi: we meet your flight and transfer you to the Oberoi hotel in Delhi for an overnight stay

6 Nov

Delhi: modes of transport from cycle rickshaws to Delhi’s modern metro take you on a sightseeing tour to discover the life and history of India’s most complex city, from the Mughal majesty to Humayun;s tomb and Qutub Minar to the bazaar of Old Delhi’s Chandni Chowk, the colonial grandeur of Lutens’New Delhi and Gandhi Smriti.

7 Nov

Agra: After breakfast, drive to Agra and check in at the Trident hotel. Later, take a guided tour around Agra Fort and visit the incomparable Taj Mahal at quieter time to see the monuments in late afternoon light.

8 Nov

Jaipur: In the early morning, visit fields across the river to see the Taj Mahal at sunrise, sublime architecture revealed in dawn light. After breakfast, drive to Jaipur, visiting the red sandstone city Fatahpur Sikri, designed ad Akbar’s new capital but abandoned after 14 years, and Abhaneri’s beautiful step well.

After reaching Jaipur and checking in Dera Mandawa, a charming heritage hotel, your evening is free to relax or visit the temple and bazaars of the Pink City.

9 Nov

Jaipur: After breakfast take a daylong sightseeing trip with our expert guide, exploring the city through history, myths and anecdotes. Ride an elephant up to the majestic hill fort of Amber to see the audience halls and beautiful mirror palace. Visit the City Palace with collections of rare manuscripts and painting, the Hawa Mahal and Jantar Mantar observatory, and Govind Dev Ji temple, where townsfolk worship daily; the attire of deities varies according to the seasons, festival, time of the day and even the weather!

10 Nov

PUSHKAR: Travel on to Pushkar, to stay for two nights in luxury tents at a camp. In the afternoon visit the camel fair, one of the greatest gatherings on earth.

11 Nov

PUSHKAR: You may climb up to one of the hilltop temples for early morning views over the fair, or take a walk around the ghats and sacred lake. Pushkar is the holiest city of Rajasthan, a temple town of priests and pilgrims, myths and legends, sacred springs and pilgrimage sites, and the renowned Brahma temple.

12 Nov

VARANASI: In the morning, drive to Jaipur airport for a flight to Varanasi, a city older than time, one of the most ancient cities in the world, the city of light, a city of death rituals yet radiant with life.

13 Nov

VARANASI: The day begins on the holy Ganges, taking a boat on the river to witness the early morning ritual baths and prayers on the ghats, a spiritual experience. After breakfast, a guided sightseeing tour takes you to visit the city, including a trip to Sarnath, where Buddha delivered his first sermon.

14 Nov

VARANASI: The day is free to explore the lanes and vibrant cultural and spiritual life of this age-old city and stroll on the ghats.

15 Nov

In the evening, we again take a riverboat for an extraordinary experience of Varanasi’s Dev Deepawali festival, on the full moon night of Kartik, when the ghats are illuminated with diyas and firebowls, innumerable ‘Diya’ earthen lamps lit by devotees float down the river and spectacular firework displays light up the sky in a mystical and moving panorama of sights you will never forget.

DELHI: Transfer to Varanasi airport to board a flight back to Delhi or your next destination.

PROPOSED HOTELS Delhi Imperial, Agra Taj View, Jaipur Dera Mandawa, Pushkar Khari Camp, Varanasi Guleria Kothi.
